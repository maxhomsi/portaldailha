Babel Guest House

A ten-suite guest house on Gigóia Island. Hand-made décor from reclaimed materials, breakfast in the courtyard and its own gastrobar. From R$ 270 a night.

Dica do Capi:

About the Stay

Babel is a ten-suite guest house known for its hand-made décor, built from reclaimed materials and repurposed objects. Every corner has its own colour, and nothing here feels like an ordinary hotel.

Sustainability is not a slogan here: a green roof, an organic vegetable garden and careful use of water and energy were part of the project from the start. Continental breakfast is included, and the house has its own bar and a garden deck on the Tijuca Lagoon. Guests tend to say the same thing — it stops feeling like Rio.

Highlights

  • ✓ Ten suites, breakfast included
  • ✓ Master and Zeus suites with a hot tub
  • ✓ Garden, lagoon deck and on-site gastrobar
  • ✓ Pets welcome (R$ 49 fee)
