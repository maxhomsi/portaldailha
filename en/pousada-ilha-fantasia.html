Pousada Ilha da Fantasia

A lagoon-side guest house minutes from Jardim Oceânico metro. Breakfast and a boat tour through the Carioca Pantanal are included in the nightly rate.

Dica do Capi:

About the Stay

Pousada Ilha da Fantasia sits on the edge of the Tijuca Lagoon, a few minutes from Jardim Oceânico metro station. It has five rooms with air conditioning, private bathroom and TV, in a house with an outdoor pool, a deck over the water, a lounge and a bar.

What sets the house apart is what already comes with the rate: breakfast, and a boat tour on the lagoon that threads the mangrove canals of the Carioca Pantanal and carries on to Praia dos Amores, with Pedra da Gávea on the horizon. It is run as a family place — the host lives there and welcomes guests himself, which is what guests mention most.
