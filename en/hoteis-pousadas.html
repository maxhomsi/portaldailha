Guesthouse or holiday home

The choice usually comes down less to the nightly rate and more to how many of you there are.

Guesthouse or boutique hotel

  • Rate per room, usually with breakfast
  • Good for a couple or two people
  • Someone at reception to help
  • Simple to book for one or two nights

Holiday home

  • Rate for the whole house, split by the group
  • Good for a family or a group of friends
  • A kitchen and outdoor space to use
  • Usually asks for a minimum number of nights

Frequently asked questions about staying on Gigóia Island

How much does a guesthouse on Gigóia Island cost?

Published rates start around R$ 250 to R$ 300 for the smaller options and go past R$ 600 for the more upmarket ones. Holiday homes are charged for the whole house, from R$ 450. The price changes with the date and the number of guests.

Is there a hotel on Gigóia Island?

No large hotel. The island has small guesthouses, one boutique hotel — the Veneza Carioca, on Ilha Primeira — and whole holiday homes.

How do you get to where you are staying?

By boat. The island has no streets and no cars: you arrive at Av. Armando Lombardi, in Barra da Tijuca, and cross over, which takes about two minutes and runs 24 hours.

Can you bring a car?

As far as the mainland, yes. There are no cars on the island. Cars stay in the private car parks around the decks on Av. Armando Lombardi.

Is there wi-fi?

Yes. The places to stay and the venues on the island offer wi-fi.

How does booking work?

In cash, Pix or card, with a 50% deposit to secure the booking. Tell Gigi the date and how many of you there are and she confirms price and availability.
