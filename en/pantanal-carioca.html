Carioca Pantanal

Explore the lagoon's rich biodiversity and connect with lush nature in the heart of Barra.

Capi's Tip:

Fique de olho no Jack!

Durante o passeio, preste bastante atenção nas margens do manguezal. Nosso famoso jacaré-de-papo-amarelo adora tomar um sol e observar os visitantes.

Duration

⏳ 45 minutes

Average Price

About the Experience

The Carioca Pantanal tour is one of the most famous itineraries in the Tijuca Lagoon region. During the trip, the boat travels through the natural canals that form the Gigóia Island archipelago, passing through the region's 10 islands and preserved mangrove areas.

Along the way, visitors can observe the rich local fauna and flora. It's common to spot caimans, herons, capybaras, and various birds that live in the lagoon's mangroves, making the tour a unique experience of contact with nature within the city of Rio de Janeiro.

What you will see

  • 🐊 Broad-snouted caimans
  • 🐾 Capybaras in their habitat
  • 🦜 Rare and migratory birds
  • 🌿 Preserved mangroves
