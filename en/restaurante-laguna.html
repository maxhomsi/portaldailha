Capi's Tip:

📸 Discover the Venue

Restaurante Laguna is considered the first establishment founded on Gigóia Island, opened in 2002. Since then, it has become a gastronomic reference in the region and one of the most traditional places for island visitors.

The restaurant offers a rustic yet sophisticated atmosphere, with wooden tables under a charming thatched roof, surrounded by gardens and with a beautiful view of Pedra da Gávea. The setting makes the space especially sought after for special occasions.

🌿 Tradition & Specialties

  • Shrimp Pastel: The famous and unmistakable pastry is considered one of the house's main highlights.
  • Stuffed Crab Shell: Authentically prepared with 100% pure meat, it is one of the most praised dishes on the menu.
  • Laguna Lobster: A sophisticated individual dish, highly sought after by those desiring a remarkable and unforgettable dining experience.

Specializing in artisanally prepared seafood, Laguna values fresh ingredients and classic recipes in an atmosphere that helps tell the story of Gigóia Island.

Special offer

While you are on the island: boat tour + lunch for R$ 100

Before or after your meal you can take the Carioca Pantanal boat tour, among caimans, capybaras and mangroves — 35 to 40 minutes, with an executive lunch included. The tour on its own is R$ 50.
