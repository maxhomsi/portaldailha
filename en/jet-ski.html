About the Experience

For those looking for more excitement on the water, jet ski rental on Gigóia Island is one of the most fun experiences in the Barra da Tijuca region. The activity allows you to explore the lagoon canals and open areas with speed, freedom, and an incredible view of the region.

Each jet ski carries up to two people, and a short briefing comes before you set off — so it works even if you have never ridden one. The life vest is included. From 30 minutes on, the time is yours: ride straight through or take turns with your group.
