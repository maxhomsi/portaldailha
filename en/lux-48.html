Comfort, practicality, and a super modern environment with a strategic location near the subway.

Capi's Tip:

About the Apartment

Lux 48 is a super modern apartment on Gigóia Island, designed to offer comfort and practicality for a short stay or weekend. The space combines a contemporary environment with a strategic location near the Jardim Oceânico subway, facilitating access to the rest of the city.

The accommodation is mainly recommended for couples or small groups who want to stay on the island with easy access to Barra da Tijuca.

Structure & Amenities

  • ✓ Up to 4 guests (1 bedroom)
  • ✓ 1 main bed and sofa bed/extra bed
  • ✓ Private balcony with BBQ and view
  • ✓ Fully equipped kitchen and living room
  • ✓ Air conditioning, Wi-Fi, and 1 full bathroom

Information

💰 Price: From R$ 250 per night.

💑 Ideal for: Couples, romantic trips, and short stays.
