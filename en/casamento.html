Weddings on Ilha da Gigóia

Five lagoon-side venues in Barra da Tijuca, where your guests arrive by boat.

Dica do Capi:

Why get married on Ilha da Gigóia

Ilha da Gigóia sits inside Barra da Tijuca, on the Tijuca Lagoon, and it has no bridge: the only way in is by boat. That single detail changes everything. The crossing takes about two minutes, but it turns your guests' arrival into part of the celebration — and gives you, in the middle of Rio de Janeiro, a wedding nobody stumbles into by accident.

The setting is water, greenery and open sky, with Pedra da Gávea in the background. And it all happens minutes from the subway and the Barra hotels.

The arrival is already part of the day

Guests cross by boat. Two minutes on the water that nobody forgets, and that produce the first photographs before the ceremony has even started.

Nature without leaving the city

Lagoon, greenery and a wide-open sunset — inside Barra da Tijuca, close to the hotels and to Jardim Oceânico subway station.

Private by nature

Because the only access is by water, the party happens away from streets, traffic and passers-by. Privacy isn't something you have to arrange: it comes with the address.

What to plan for

Guest transport is the first item on the list, not the last. Agree with the venue how the crossing will work. A chartered boat on a set schedule is far calmer than leaving each guest to sort it out on the spot, especially at night and in formal clothes.

Cars stay on the mainland. There is no road access to the island. Guests leave the car in the private car parks around Av. Armando Lombardi, or arrive by subway at Jardim Oceânico station, less than a five-minute walk from the boarding deck.

Check the sunset time before fixing the ceremony hour. Outdoors and facing the water, late afternoon is the island's best light — and it shifts considerably between summer and winter.

Your suppliers cross by boat too. Cake, flowers, sound, staging: everything arrives over the water. That isn't a problem, but loading and unloading have to be agreed in advance. Ask every supplier about it before you book them.

Think about guests with reduced mobility. Boarding and landing happen on a deck. Tell the venue in advance so someone can assist those guests on arrival.

Have a covered plan B. The island's charm is the open sky; the trade-off is rain. Ask each venue what the indoor alternative is and how many people it holds.

Frequently asked questions

How do guests get to Ilha da Gigóia?

By boat. The island has no bridge. Crossings leave from the decks on Av. Armando Lombardi, in Barra da Tijuca, and take about two minutes. For weddings, the venue usually arranges the boats on a set schedule.

Is there parking on the island?

Not on the island, because there are no cars. Guests leave the car in the private car parks around the decks, on the mainland, or arrive at Jardim Oceânico subway station, less than a five-minute walk away.

How many guests fit?

It varies a great deal from venue to venue, from intimate receptions to large parties, and it changes with the format: ceremony, seated dinner or standing reception. Talk to us and we'll point you to the right venue for your numbers.

What is the best time of year?

The island hosts weddings all year round. Summer brings longer days and more movement; autumn and winter are mild, the island is quieter, and dates are usually easier to book.

Can the ceremony and the party be in the same place?

At several of the venues, yes. That is one of the real advantages of marrying here, since it avoids moving guests between two addresses over the water.

Can guests stay overnight on the island?

Yes. The island has guesthouses and holiday rentals, and some event venues offer accommodation at the same address as the party.

Capacity, prices and available dates belong to each venue and change through the year. Talk to us for the current information.
