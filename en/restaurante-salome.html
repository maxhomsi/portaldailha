Capi's Tip:

📸 Discover the Venue

Salomé al Mare is one of the pillars of the Ilha Primeira gastronomic hub. With a cozy environment by the lagoon, the space is perfect for those seeking to relax and eat well surrounded by a privileged view.

The house is celebrated for its rigorous selection of seafood, prepared with the freshness that the island's climate demands.

🐚 Mandatory Stops

  • Shrimp Pastel: Crispy and generously filled, it is noted by many as one of the best on the entire Island.
  • Seafood Platter: A hearty and complete option, ideal for sharing among friends or family while enjoying the lagoon breeze.

With attentive service and dishes that mark the memory, Salomé al Mare is an indispensable stop for those wishing to explore the best of lagoon cuisine.
